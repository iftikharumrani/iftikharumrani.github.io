About

My background brings together computing, networking, telecom, cybersecurity, and applied systems work. I enjoy explaining technical subjects in a clear and practical way, using examples, labs, and projects so students can connect the theory with real problems.

Along with teaching, I have been involved in course development, assessment, curriculum support, accreditation work, project supervision, and departmental activities. I like a classroom where students feel supported, engaged, and challenged.

I joined Walton Institute as a PhD research student in the CONNECT Project, funded by Research Ireland (formerly Science Foundation Ireland), in February 2023. My research aims to improve the efficiency and reliability of UAV operations while minimizing potential threats, using deep learning based security measures.

Areas of expertise: Artificial Intelligence and machine learning, cybersecurity, cyber physical systems, UAV security, and computer networks and telecommunication.

Research

PhD in Computing, South East Technological University.

My research studies how GNSS spoofing and false data injection can covertly mislead the navigation of a UAV, using a simulation testbed built on PX4, Gazebo, and ROS. This matters because a UAV that trusts a false position can be steered off its route without raising an alarm. I am currently analysing digital twin simulation results for machine learning based detection of covert spoofing from inconsistencies in onboard data, so that such attacks can be caught early and UAVs stay reliable.

Where the work is done

Programmable Autonomous System Lab, Walton Institute, SETU. Project name: UAVSec.

What I built and used

  • UAV software stack integration using Gazebo, PX4, ROS/MAVROS, QGroundControl, and MAVLink.
  • Testing and debugging of drone behaviour under normal and degraded navigation conditions.
  • Analysis of flight logs and telemetry using Python and UAV analysis tools.
  • Design and evaluation of practical UAV test scenarios, including faulty and spoofed cases.
  • Documentation of test cases, observed behaviour, failure modes, and validation results.

Topics of the published and presented work

  • A unified threat taxonomy (U-TUAST) and cross layer survey of UAV cybersecurity challenges.
  • False data injection attacks on UAV navigation and obstacle avoidance, including GPS and LiDAR spoofing, in a simulation testbed.
  • Trust in AI driven decision making for cyber physical systems.
  • Stealthy GNSS spoofing and estimator drift in UAV navigation systems (poster, CONNECT Plenary 2025).
